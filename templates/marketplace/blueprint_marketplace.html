About blueprint packs

Each pack defines a policy_snapshot (terminology, grading, admissions, features). Applying it creates a PolicyBundle for the selected school and sets TenantBlueprint.active_bundle so get_effective_policy(school) merges from that bundle when POLICY_USE_BUNDLES is True. Use Preview before Apply; use Revert to restore a previous bundle.
