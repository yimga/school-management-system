Trust state

Compatibility clues

Sandbox-first activation

Rollback and safety posture

  • Sandbox install keeps rollout deliberate instead of instantly live.
  • Scope consent and marketplace governance remain visible after install.
  • Operators can inspect sandbox installations before promotion.
  • Kill switch and approval states stay separate from school-facing install UX.
