{% trans "Migration data-processing consent" %}

{% blocktrans %}Your school is moving its records into RunMyCampus. As part of this move, certain information about your child — including name, enrollment status, grades, attendance, and contact details — will be copied from your school's current system into RunMyCampus.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What we're asking" %}

{% blocktrans %}Before the move begins, we ask each parent or legal guardian to confirm that they understand and consent to this data transfer. This consent is recorded for legal and audit purposes.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What data is involved" %}

  • {% trans "Your child's name and date of birth" %}
  • {% trans "Enrollment status and historical academic records" %}
  • {% trans "Attendance and discipline history" %}
  • {% trans "Contact information for you and your child" %}

{% trans "Scope of access" %}

{% blocktrans %}Only RunMyCampus engineers acting under the school's written authorization (the Migration Authorization Agreement) will handle this data, and only for the purpose of migrating it. We apply data minimization — we do not extract fields the school did not request.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Data minimization and retention" %}

{% blocktrans %}There is no retention beyond migration. Working copies of the extracted data are destroyed once the import has been validated by your school. Your child's records remain in your school's official system of record.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Your rights" %}

{% blocktrans %}You have the right to withdraw at any time within 90 days of giving consent. You also have the data subject rights granted by FERPA, COPPA (if your child is under 13), and the GDPR (if you reside in the European Union): the right to know what we hold, to correct it, and to request deletion subject to the school's record- keeping obligations.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "How to contact us" %}

{% blocktrans %}For questions about this consent, contact your school's data-protection officer. For technical questions about RunMyCampus, contact privacy@runmycampus.com.{% endblocktrans %}
