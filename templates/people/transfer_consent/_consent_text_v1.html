Student transfer consent (v1)

You are being asked to consent to the transfer of your child's school record from {{ case.source_school.name }} to {{ case.target_school.name }} on the RunMyCampus platform.

  • Scope of access: only the record categories listed on this page move; financial balances stay with the current school unless separately agreed.
  • Data minimization: the receiving school gets the student's enrollment identity, guardians, attendance and academic history — nothing else.

  • Right to decline: declining cancels the transfer; nothing is sent.
