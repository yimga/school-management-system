{% load i18n %} {% comment %} BAND 3 — the schedule, and what it actually produces. WHAT WAS WRONG. The old panel showed a SENTENCE ("Every 30 minutes, 06:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. At 22:00, every day.") and nothing else, so a school could not see that those two rules leave Sunday with a fourteen-hour hole, that they overlap at 18:00, or that the hourly check-in was doing most of the real work. The add-rule editor was a raw {{ form.as_p }}: eight stacked controls, no preview, and a "Times" text box meaningful for only one of the two "Type" values with nothing on screen saying which. Below an
, a SECOND form with a SECOND save button set the check-in ceiling — which also changes how often the box syncs. So "how often does this box sync?" was answered in two places, and neither mentioned the other. WHY THE STRIP IS SERVER-RENDERED. apps/sync_engine/schedule.py opens by saying the label on the screen and the moment the scheduler acts have to be the same computation. A strip drawn by a re-implementation in the browser would drift the first time either changed, and it would drift silently — a wrong strip still looks like a strip. So this renders from schedule_policy.coverage_for(), and the live preview POSTs a candidate rule set back to sync_schedule_preview rather than computing anything client-side. Progressive enhancement: strip, rules, next-five and both forms all work with JavaScript off. What JS adds is the hover readout and the preview-before-save. {% endcomment %}
