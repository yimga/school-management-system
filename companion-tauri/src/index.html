RunMyCampus Migration Companion

This appliance handshakes with the RunMyCampus platform and ingests canonical CSV files the operator manually exports from their SIS's own export UI. We do NOT log into PowerSchool / Blackbaud / Veracross / Alma / FACTS / Skyward on your behalf.

Step 1 — Login to RunMyCampus

Step 2 — Migration Authorization Agreement

Step 3 — Pick canonical CSV

Open your SIS in your own browser, sign in, and use its export UI to save a CSV to disk. Then pick the file here.

Step 4 — Done

The migration bundle is queued. Visit the RunMyCampus operator portal to advance through the wizard.
