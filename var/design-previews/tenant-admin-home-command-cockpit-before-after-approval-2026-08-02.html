Audit first · evidence-backed

One surface. Seven boxes. Too much scroll.

The Admin Home at /authentication/backend/ is functionally rich but visually fragmented: every module is its own bordered island, the same "what do I do next" is repeated three ways, the most important button looks like a caption, and developer scaffolding leaks into the page. The target folds the whole surface into one command cockpit — every real action preserved, nothing above the fold lost to scroll.

Approval required before production visual changes
Stacked-island composition

Next-action hero, role-home, mission band, journey, dashboard-style and the checklist link are separate border+radius+margin cards. Each repeats chrome; the useful content is a fraction of the height it occupies.

The key action is a bare link

.rmc-setup-surface__all is display:inline-block with only a brand colour — no affordance. "Open full activation checklist" is the one thing a new admin must click, and it looks unclickable, last on the page.

Invisible narration (contrast, not opacity)

The mission-season hint ("Prioritize admissions, capacity, and guardian onboarding") is .text-muted gray on a ~10% peach-over-white cream band → ≈1.4:1. Present in the DOM, unreadable on screen. Fix keeps the copy and gives it accessible ink.

Developer labels on the customer surface

ROLE_HOME, Job to be done, Main question, Primary action are internal framing that shipped as visible copy. A head teacher should never see the scaffold.

Triple-answered "next step"

The next action appears in the 92% hero, again in role-home, and again in the mission band — three components competing to tell the admin the same thing.

Nothing real is being removed

Every route (Configuration, School Studio, Configure hub, Finance), the readiness ring, the four journey phases and the theme picker survive — re-homed into one bounded, scannable cockpit.

One pattern · whole platform

Fix the grammar, not one screen.

Admin Home is the exemplar, but the stacked-island + bare-link + faded-copy pattern recurs wherever a surface was assembled from many partials. After you approve the cockpit grammar, the same three rules are applied end-to-end across tenant and operator — audit first, then fix, test, re-audit.

tenant-wide + operator · no exceptions
Rule 1 — ConsolidateSibling islands that share a job fold into one bounded card with hairline-separated zones. No box-in-a-box.
Rule 2 — Real buttonsAny primary/secondary action is a real button with affordance, hoisted into the header — never a bare inline link at the bottom.
Rule 3 — No scaffold, no ghostsDeveloper labels removed; every line of copy meets ≥4.5:1 contrast or is cut. −40% scroll & clicks is the floor, not the goal.
