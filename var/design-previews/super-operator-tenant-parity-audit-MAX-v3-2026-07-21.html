04

MAX consistency machinery (so you stop repeating yourself)

These are the “set and forget” enforcers. Once green, wallpaper badges and uneven twins cannot quietly return.

Wallpaper badge scanner

Fails CI on static Operational, hardcoded catalog ready, and synonym status labels not in the locked vocabulary.

Twin masthead contract

Operator vs tenant Money/Setup/Mission must expose the same slots: title, chips, primary, freshness, optional Why.

Band-count / fold SLA

Max 2 chrome bands above [data-rmc-work-root]. Primary work within ~320px. Cramping becomes a gate, not a debate.

Visibility / anti-bleed

No text parent with overflow:hidden + sticky. Chip contrast AA. Grids use minmax(0,1fr). Host color only on frame chrome.
