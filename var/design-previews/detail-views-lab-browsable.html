Detail & Profile Views Lab — Surface 8

The journey is nav → list → detail. Detail pages already have a key-value grammar (.rmc-kv) and a section-nav observer (rmc-section-nav.js) — but copy-to-clipboard is wired for the MFA secret only, section navs must be hand-authored link by link, and empty fields render as a blank gap. The detail engine enhances field values in place: copy, mailto/tel, em-dash — and auto-fills an author-placed section nav.
