How it attaches (the architectural lever)

Same pattern that made the data-table engine reach 377 pages with zero per-page edits — pick the hook once:

A · Auto-attach to every <form method="post"> B · Only forms using the shared form partials / .rmc-form wrapper C · Opt-in per form

Recommended: A with smart skips + opt-out — auto-enhance all POST forms, but skip search/filter/autosubmit/single-button forms automatically, and honour data-rmc-form-enhance="0". Maximum reach, no surprises.
