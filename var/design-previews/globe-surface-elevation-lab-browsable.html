Six simple upgrades for the Global Footprint canvas on /super/. Each mock keeps the 3D globe as hero — we only rethink the void, chrome, and side panel. Pick one (or combine). Open this file in your browser; use the pills below to compare concepts.

Concept D — Constellation mode

Updated 3:11:53 PM

Idea

  • The fleet count becomes part of the globe — not a sidebar headline.
  • Giant translucent “2” sits behind the sphere; region chips orbit the perimeter.
  • Side panel optional or hidden on wide screens.

Why it’s simple

  • One visual anchor — operators see scale instantly.
  • Orbit chips replace legend rows (same click → fly-to behavior).
  • Feels premium with almost no new logic.bold
