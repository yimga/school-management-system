Post-approval implementation contract

  • Route-scoped semantic classes; no global override of .grid, .card, or .panel.
  • Desktop catalog/inspector split; single column at 1024px and below.
  • Real Preview and Simulate query paths remain owned by the Django view.
  • Apply, approval request, deactivate, and rollback stay real POST forms with CSRF.
  • Tenant surface never exposes platform registry, fleet, Studio, or cross-school controls.
  • Audit all 195 operational workbenches again after implementation and capture real-host screenshots at 1440/1024/768/390 in light and dark.
