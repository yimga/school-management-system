Why this is the right fix

One rule was quietly defeating two of Unfold’s native layouts at once:

  • .form-row{display:flex;flex-direction:column} beat field-row lg:grid-cols-2/3 → grouped fields stacked full-width (long pages).
  • the same selector matched a tabular inline’s <tr class="form-row"> → the row shattered into a stacked column (bleed + wasted height).

The upgrade doesn’t bolt on new chrome — it stops fighting the framework and adds a premium finish on top (scroll panel, sticky header, zebra, tighter rhythm). Scoped to [data-rmc-shell-root="django-admin"] so tenant and operator both get it, on every model — not just add-student.
