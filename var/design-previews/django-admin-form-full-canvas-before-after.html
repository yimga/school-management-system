What was wrong

Older `admin-cp-parity.css` rules used higher-specificity `!important` selectors and kept forms capped by `--rmc-backoffice-form-max`.

What the patch changes

The final contract now targets the exact Django form and table markers: `premium-form-frame`, `native-table-scroll`, and `django-admin` shell root.

Why it should render now

The CSS URL and service worker cache were bumped again, and the new selectors are specific enough to beat the old narrow form cap.
