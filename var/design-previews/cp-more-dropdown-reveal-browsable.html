Header “More ▾” dropdown — before vs. after the fix

On /super/ and /admin/ the header folds nav items 6+ into a native <details> “More ▾” dropdown. Clicking it does toggle open — but the menu was being clipped because Chromium coerces overflow-y:visible back to clip when overflow-x:clip is set on .cp-header. Click “⋯ More” in each panel below to feel the difference.
