# Plan policy — non-negotiable, due now

**Effective:** All plans, architecture docs, and execution checklists.

## Rule

- **Everything is non-negotiable and due now.** There are no "optional," "deferred," "backlog," or "save for later" items. There is no "out of scope" unless a formal platform decision explicitly removes a capability from scope with date and owner.
- **Implementation:** Every scoped item must be either (1) **implemented in code** and verified, or (2) **scheduled with an owner and target date** and implemented by that date. Nothing remains unscheduled or "when we get to it."
- **Optionals = required.** Any item previously labeled "optional" is treated as required and due now; implement and integrate into the platform.
- **Consistency:** Docs and code must not use "deferred," "backlog," "optional," or "out of scope" in a way that implies work is abandoned or indefinitely postponed. Use "required, due now" or "required (owner X, target date Y)."
