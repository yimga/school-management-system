# RunMyCampus Marketing Visual Asset and AI Prompt Pack

**All items in this document are required. Nothing is optional or backlogged. Aligned with [docs/PLAN_POLICY.md](PLAN_POLICY.md).**

Purpose: Create enterprise-grade marketing visuals for runmycampus.com so the platform communicates global scale, platform architecture, product usability, AI intelligence, migration superiority, and modular ecosystem—positioning RunMyCampus as the Salesforce / Shopify / AWS of education infrastructure.

---

## 1. Core visual asset categories (all mandatory)

The marketing site uses four primary asset classes. All are required.

1. **High-fidelity product mockups** — Admin dashboard, teacher dashboard, parent portal, student portal, marketplace, AI insights, migration studio, workflow builder.
2. **Platform architecture diagrams** — Platform layers, Migration Cloud pipeline, Marketplace ecosystem, AI intelligence layer, integration architecture.
3. **Short AI-generated motion pieces** — Hero loop, migration explainer, onboarding journey, workflow simulation, global network motion.
4. **Human-context visuals** — Teacher/parent/student/admin outcomes and relief; globally diverse; not cheesy stock.

**Trust rule:** Frame all visuals as "product preview," "platform concept," "example workflow," "coming soon," or "early access preview." Never use fake customer logos, fake testimonials, fake usage numbers, or fake case studies.

## 9. How a school starts (user-facing only)

Aligned with [HOW_A_SCHOOL_STARTS.md](HOW_A_SCHOOL_STARTS.md). Three phases in marketing language only; no implementation or internal logic.

1. **Sign up and complete a guided setup wizard** to get started—choose region, plan or trial, and see your upgrade path.
2. **Brand your school** with templates, your logo and colors, or by importing from your existing website; see a live preview and use the design studio for more.
3. **Choose your plan and features** and see live previews before you launch.

Marketing implements this via: homepage "Launch your school in minutes" section with `school_in_a_box_flow_image_url` and CTA to `onboard_wizard`; get-started three steps; onboarding wizard (region, plan comparison, template gallery, import-from-URL). All copy and assets stay capability-focused.

## 10. Production workflow (mandatory)

- **Stage 1:** Define folders (hero, UI mockups, diagrams, videos, mobile previews, lifestyle/human, marketplace, migration).
- **Stage 2:** Generate rough concepts with AI.
- **Stage 3:** Curate; reject non–premium, non-coherent, non-minimal, or non–enterprise-grade options.
- **Stage 4:** Refine in design tools (colors, typography, shadows, device framing, spacing).
- **Stage 5:** Section-specific exports (hero, half-width, mobile crop, social share, blog thumbnail, pitch deck).
- **Stage 6:** Animate top-value assets only (no optional skip).

## 11. Design rules

- **Whitespace first;** avoid clutter.
- **Enterprise trust aesthetic:** Inspired by Salesforce, Shopify, Stripe, Linear, Notion.
- **Color discipline:** Primary: Deep Indigo, Oxygen Blue. Accent: Teal, Soft Gray.
- **Typography:** Inter; SF Pro–style system font.

## 12. Filler strategies (no empty sections)

When a section would otherwise be text-only, use one of:

1. **Architecture card strip** — Icon + headline + one-line explanation + small mockup crop.
2. **Side-by-side visual narrative** — Strong headline and copy on one side; product mockup or diagram on the other.
3. **Motion-backed section** — Short loop + translucent overlay + clean copy + CTA.
4. **Proof block** — Visual metric concept, diagram, checklist, or "how it works" steps.
5. **Workflow band** — Trigger → action → outcome → role involved.

Every section must have at least one of: product mockup, system diagram, or motion.
