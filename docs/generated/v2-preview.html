A day at one school

A Tuesday at Cedar Ridge Academy.

Five people, six bells, one quiet system. Here's how a real day actually moves.

  1. 7:42a.m. Before the bell

    Olu opens the kitchen window. RunMyCampus has already staged today's plan: cover for Ms Akande, a reminder out to the Y9 trip families, and the four parents whose fees fell due overnight.

  2. 8:15a.m. First bell

    Year 5B's roll marks itself in nine seconds. Maya is present. Aisha is absent — her mum is already in the inbox, no chase call needed.

  3. 10:30a.m. Stand-up

    Permission slips for Y9 history clear in five minutes. Twenty-seven of twenty-eight back. The last family gets a quiet nudge — no admin, no spreadsheet, no rerun.

  4. 12:00p.m. Lunch

    The kitchen counts 412 hot meals. Three dietary flags are surfaced for chef. The finance ledger reconciles before the bell — no end-of-day catch-up.

  5. 3:15p.m. Last bell

    Forty-seven buses leave. Imani's phone hums quietly on the train home: 'Maya is on the 47.' No app to open. No worry to swallow.

  6. 5:30p.m. Day closed

    Olu closes her laptop. Tomorrow's plan is already staged — register sheets, the Parents' evening rota, and the mid-term reports that go out at three.

Fictional school for illustration. The rhythm is real — these are the surfaces every RunMyCampus tenant runs daily.
