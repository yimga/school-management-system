# Hiring plan (RunMyCampus)

Hiring is **out of the codebase**; triggers below are for **operational** decisions, not a promise to the team.

## Stage 1: 0–20 live customers (founder-led)

- **You:** sales, first deploys, and escalations.  
- **+1 (optional, part-time):** support/ops—tickets, scheduling, and smoke-test reruns. **Trigger:** >10 hours/week on repeatable login/DNS/pilot tasks.

## Stage 2: 20–100

- **Sales rep** (or founder + inside sales) — one role, not a “team of five.” **Trigger:** Demo calendar consistently full; deals lost only to **capacity**.  
- **Onboarding specialist** — runs activation checklist, trains CCC basics. **Trigger:** >3 parallel pilots.  
- **Support** (shared with onboarding at first) — same person ok until ticket volume forces split.

## Stage 3: 100–500

- **Customer success** (light): renewal risk, QBR, usage from activation data—not vanity analytics.  
- **Product engineer** (one) — **internal** platform work and tenant edge cases, not feature churn. **Trigger:** weekly engineering firefighting from one-off schools.

## Stage 4: 500+

- **Head of growth** (or rev ops) for pipeline + partner hygiene.  
- **Enterprise sales** (one) for multi-campus; founder stays on flagship deals.  
- **Platform engineering** as needed for SLO, backup, and multi-region *if* you choose that path.
