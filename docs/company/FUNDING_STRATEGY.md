# Funding strategy (principles, not deal advice)

**This is not legal or investment advice.** It aligns **how** to talk about RunMyCampus with investors *if* you choose to raise, without inventing metrics.

## Bootstrapped path

- **When it fits:** You can reach **profitability** on services + narrow niche without dilution; you control tempo.  
- **Trade-off:** Slower market expansion; enterprise procurement may want vendor scale you have not yet built.

## If you raise: metrics to prepare (real, not pitch fiction)

- **MRR/ARR** from actual subscriptions or contracts you can show in **bank + billing records** (not a spreadsheet).  
- **Gross and net revenue churn** (even rough) for pilot→paid.  
- **Logo risk:** Do not name schools in decks without written permission; use “private K–12, N students” if needed.

## Stages (typical, region-dependent)

- **Pre-seed:** Prototype + first paid pilots; small round for runway to repeatable demo.  
- **Seed:** Repeatable GTM, 2–3 people possible; use `HIRING_PLAN.md` triggers.  
- **Series A:** You need a **proven** channel (referral, partner, or inside sales) and a path to 10× without breaking support.

## How to position the product (honest)

- **Infrastructure for school operations** — control plane, evidence, and tenant-scoped data.  
- **Not** “we replace the LMS/ERP” unless you truly do in your target segment.
