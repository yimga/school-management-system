# First 50 paying schools (execution plan)

**Target profile (default):** Private K–12, ~200–1,500 students, one or few campuses, leadership reachable on LinkedIn or referral.

## Weekly throughput (start here)

| Activity | Qty (week 1–8) |
|----------|----------------|
| New conversations started | 15–25 |
| Demos completed | 3–5 |
| Pilots offered | 1–2 |
| Pilots in onboarding | 1 (until you have coverage) |

Adjust down if you are solo; the **bottleneck** is founder calendar, not lead list size.

## Daily (60–90 min)

- 30 min: outbound (LinkedIn + email) using `OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md`.  
- 20 min: follow-ups.  
- 20 min: **one** deep task (demo prep, support for hottest pilot, or deploy verification).

## Conversion steps

1. **Identified** → **Contacted** (reply or booked).  
2. **Demo** (45 min, `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`).  
3. **Pilot offer** in writing: scope, duration, what “green” means (`ENTERPRISE_PILOT_PLAN.md`).  
4. **Onboarding** with `/siteconfig/onboarding/` and smoke subset.  
5. **Active** = paying per your `Plan` / contract—not “using the demo”.

**Payment** is an **out-of-product** process unless you have wired a processor; do not document fake Stripe.

## Onboarding to payment (operational)

- **Commercial:** quote + invoice path you actually use.  
- **Product:** `FIRST_CUSTOMER_ONBOARDING.md` for tenant + entitlements.  
- **Handoff:** named success contact for 30 days.

## Early customer success (lightweight)

- **30-day check:** activation %, one report, one leadership touchpoint.  
- **Rescue:** if activation <30% at day 30, one remediation workshop—then re-scope or exit.
