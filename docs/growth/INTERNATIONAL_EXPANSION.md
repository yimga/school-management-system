# International expansion (playbook, not a guarantee)

**You must verify law, tax, and data residency with counsel** for each market. The product is **configurable** for regions, but *you* decide where to offer service.

## Target regions (examples to prioritize research)

- **English-speaking** private or independent schools first if your docs and support are English-first.  
- **African private markets:** Good fit for tenant-isolated, mobile-friendly portals—**if** you can support time zones and local payment rails.  
- **UK / US:** Strong procurement; expect privacy and child-safety questions early.

## Localization (product-facing)

- **i18n** and locale where already in Django templates and settings—do not claim full translation coverage until shipping real strings.  
- **Date and grading:** Region packs / grading scales in product must match what you can **configure** in tenant—see your academics and siteconfig runbooks.

## Compliance (high level)

- Map **DPA, subprocessors, and data location** to what the deployment **actually** does (hosting region, DB, backups).  
- Do **not** claim certifications you have not passed; point to your **Trust** materials if you publish them.

## GTM by region

- **Partner-first** where direct outbound is slow (use one association or group per country).  
- **Direct** where you have founder time and a narrow niche.  
- **Sequencing:** one country to **operational** depth before the next; avoid 10 shallow launches.
